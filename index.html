01 / THE APOLOGY

A letter I wanted to write with care

I'm sorry.
And I mean it.

My Dhanashree,

I've been thinking about us, about you, and about the moments when I could have handled things better. I don't want to hide behind excuses or make this about how bad I feel. I want to acknowledge that my words and actions can affect you, and I'm genuinely sorry for the hurt I've caused.

You deserve to feel heard, respected, and safe telling me how you feel — even when the conversation is difficult. I should never make you feel like your feelings are too much, or that you have to explain your hurt perfectly before I take it seriously.

I can't rewrite a moment by sending a beautiful message. I can only own my part, listen without getting defensive, and try to show you through my actions that I have understood. That's the part that matters most, and I know it takes more than words.

I'm not asking you to forget what happened or to be okay before you're ready. You don't owe me an immediate reply, reassurance, or forgiveness. I just wanted you to hear a sincere apology from me, without pressure attached to it.

I'm sorry for the hurt. I'm sorry for the moments I didn't show up the way you needed. And I'm sorry for any time my pride got in the way of listening properly.

You matter to me — your feelings, your peace, and your happiness. I want to treat all of that with the care it deserves.

With love and accountability,Shardul♡
